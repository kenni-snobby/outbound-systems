# ICP Research Playbook

## Purpose

Great outbound starts before outreach.

The quality of conversations is directly influenced by the quality of targeting.

---

## Objective

Identify prospects that:

- Have a relevant problem
- Can buy
- Need a solution now
- Align with the Ideal Customer Profile

---

## Research Framework

### Company Research

Understand:

- Industry
- Revenue Model
- Growth Stage
- Team Size
- Market Position

Questions:

- What business are they in?
- How do they generate revenue?
- What challenges are common in their market?

---

## Growth Signals

Look for:

- Hiring activity
- Funding announcements
- Market expansion
- Product launches
- Leadership hires

These signals often indicate change.

Change creates buying opportunities.

---

## Technology Signals

Identify:

- CRM Platform
- Marketing Stack
- Sales Tools
- Automation Tools

Questions:

- What systems are already in place?
- What gaps may exist?

---

## Pain Signal Research

Common indicators:

- Slow growth
- Pipeline issues
- Forecasting challenges
- Sales inefficiencies
- CRM adoption problems

---

## Stakeholder Research

Identify:

- Decision Maker
- Influencer
- Champion
- End User

Understand:

- Goals
- KPIs
- Challenges

---

## Account Prioritization

### Tier 1

Highest ICP alignment.

Immediate outreach.

---

### Tier 2

Good fit.

Nurture and monitor.

---

### Tier 3

Low fit.

Minimal investment.

---

## Research Sources

- LinkedIn
- Company Website
- Job Posts
- News Articles
- Funding Announcements
- Industry Reports

---

## Common Mistakes

- Researching too little
- Researching too much
- Ignoring buying signals
- Prioritizing personalization over relevance

---

## Key Principle

Research should improve conversations.

Not delay them.
