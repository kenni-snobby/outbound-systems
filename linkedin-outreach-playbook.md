# LinkedIn Outreach Playbook

## Purpose

LinkedIn outreach is not about pitching.

It is about creating familiarity before creating conversations.

The goal is to become recognizable before becoming relevant.

---

## The LinkedIn Framework

Visibility → Familiarity → Trust → Conversation

---

## Step 1: Visibility

Actions:

- Optimize profile
- Publish insights
- Engage with ICP content
- Build industry presence

Goal:

Be seen before outreach.

---

## Step 2: Familiarity

Actions:

- Profile visits
- Content engagement
- Thoughtful comments
- Connection requests

Goal:

Reduce coldness.

---

## Step 3: Trust

Actions:

- Share valuable insights
- Participate in conversations
- Demonstrate expertise

Goal:

Earn credibility.

---

## Step 4: Conversation

Actions:

- Send relevant messages
- Reference business context
- Ask thoughtful questions

Goal:

Create dialogue.

---

## Connection Request Framework

Keep it simple.

Example:

"Hi Sarah,

Enjoyed your recent post on RevOps.

Would love to connect and follow your insights."

---

## First Message Framework

Avoid:

- Pitching immediately
- Long introductions
- Product explanations

Focus on:

- Shared context
- Relevant observations
- Genuine curiosity

---

## Engagement Strategy

Prioritize:

- Author-replied threads
- Industry discussions
- ICP content

Goal:

Build visibility through participation.

---

## Success Metrics

Track:

- Connection Acceptance Rate
- Reply Rate
- Conversation Rate
- Meetings Generated
- Pipeline Created

---

## Common Mistakes

- Selling too early
- Generic connection requests
- No engagement strategy
- Inconsistent activity

---

## Key Principle

People respond more often to familiar names than perfect messages.
