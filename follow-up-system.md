# Follow-Up System

## Purpose

Most opportunities are not lost because of poor outreach.

They are lost because follow-up stops too early.

The objective of follow-up is to remain visible without becoming annoying.

---

## Why Follow-Up Matters

Prospects are busy.

Silence does not always mean lack of interest.

Common reasons for non-response:

- Timing
- Internal priorities
- Decision delays
- Competing initiatives

---

## Follow-Up Principles

1. Add value with every touch.
2. Avoid "just checking in."
3. Stay relevant.
4. Respect the prospect's time.
5. Be persistent, not pushy.

---

## Recommended Follow-Up Sequence

### Day 1

Initial Outreach

Goal:

Start conversation.

---

### Day 3

Follow-Up #1

Add:

- New insight
- Industry observation
- Relevant resource

---

### Day 7

Follow-Up #2

Add:

- Case study
- Success story
- Practical example

---

### Day 14

Follow-Up #3

Add:

- Strategic question
- Business observation

---

### Day 21

Follow-Up #4

Send:

Value-focused closeout message.

Example:

"Happy to close the loop if this isn't a priority right now."

---

## Multi-Channel Follow-Up

Combine:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- Phone
- Referrals

Goal:

Increase visibility.

---

## Success Metrics

Track:

- Reply Rate
- Positive Reply Rate
- Meeting Conversion Rate
- Pipeline Created

---

## Common Mistakes

- Following up without value
- Stopping too early
- Sending identical messages
- Being overly aggressive

---

## Follow-Up Rule

Every follow-up should answer:

Why should the prospect care today?

---

## Key Principle

Consistency creates opportunities.

Most outbound success comes from thoughtful persistence.
