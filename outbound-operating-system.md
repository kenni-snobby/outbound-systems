# Outbound Operating System

## Purpose

Outbound should be treated as a system.

Not a sequence of random activities.

The goal is predictable pipeline creation.

---

## Core Components

### Targeting

Focus:

- ICP Alignment
- Buying Signals
- Strategic Fit

---

### Research

Understand:

- Business Model
- Growth Stage
- Revenue Challenges
- Market Position

---

### Messaging

Structure:

Pain → Impact → Insight → Conversation

Goal:

Create relevance.

---

### Multi-Channel Execution

Channels:

- LinkedIn
- Email
- Calls
- Referrals

---

### Follow-Up

Rule:

Persistence without annoyance.

Typical sequence:

- Day 1
- Day 3
- Day 7
- Day 14

---

## Success Metrics

Track:

- Reply Rate
- Meeting Rate
- SQL Creation
- Pipeline Generated
- Revenue Influence

---

## Common Mistakes

- Weak targeting
- Generic messaging
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Channel dependency

---

## Key Principle

Outbound success is rarely a volume problem.

It is usually a relevance problem.
