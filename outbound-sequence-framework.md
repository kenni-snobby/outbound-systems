# Outbound Sequence Framework

## Purpose

Outbound success depends on consistency, relevance, and timing.

The objective is not to send more messages.

The objective is to create more conversations.

---

## Sequence Design Principles

1. Multi-channel beats single-channel.
2. Relevance beats personalization.
3. Persistence beats one-touch outreach.
4. Conversations beat pitches.

---

## Example 10-Day Sequence

### Day 1

LinkedIn Profile Visit

Goal:

Create familiarity.

---

### Day 2

Connection Request

Goal:

Open a communication channel.

---

### Day 3

Personalized Email #1

Structure:

Pain → Impact → Insight

Goal:

Start a conversation.

---

### Day 5

Follow-Up Email #1

Focus:

New insight or observation.

Goal:

Increase response probability.

---

### Day 7

LinkedIn Engagement

Actions:

- Like content
- Leave thoughtful comment
- Engage naturally

Goal:

Build trust.

---

### Day 8

Phone Outreach

Goal:

Direct qualification.

---

### Day 10

Final Follow-Up

Focus:

Value-based closeout.

Example:

"Happy to close the loop if this isn't a priority right now."

---

## Messaging Rules

Avoid:

- Generic templates
- Feature-heavy pitches
- Long introductions

Focus on:

- Business outcomes
- Pain points
- Revenue impact

---

## Success Metrics

Track:

- Open Rate
- Reply Rate
- Positive Reply Rate
- Meeting Rate
- Pipeline Created

---

## Common Mistakes

- Giving up too early
- Over-personalization
- Weak targeting
- No follow-up process

---

## Key Principle

Most opportunities are lost because outreach stops too early.

Not because prospects were never interested.
